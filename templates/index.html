Signed in to Podimo with Apple, Facebook or Google?

Those buttons never give Podimo a password, and this tool needs one. Set a normal password on your account first — you can keep using the social buttons in the Podimo app afterwards.

  1. Sign in to Podimo the way you normally do.
  2. Open your account settings and note the email address on the account. With Sign in with Apple it looks like abcdefg@privaterelay.appleid.com.
  3. Sign out of Podimo.
  4. On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password and enter that email address.
  5. Open the reset link Podimo emails you and choose a password.
  6. Sign in once with that email address and password, skipping the Apple, Facebook and Google buttons, to confirm the pair works.
  7. Enter the same email address and password in the form above.
